About

I earned my PhD in Mathematics-Statistics in 2013, building on an MSc (2009) and BSc (1994) in the same field. Since then I've worked at the Greater Amman Municipality across knowledge management, indicator production, and statistical training, while independently supporting postgraduate researchers across the region.

My work spans quantitative and qualitative analysis — from designing surveys in KoBo Toolbox and Google Forms to running the full analysis pipeline in SPSS, Minitab, AMOS, and NVivo — and issuing the statistical reports that back Amman's official indicator sets, including the City Prosperity Index and the Urban Monitoring Framework.
